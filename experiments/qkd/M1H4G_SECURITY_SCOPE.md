# M1.H4.G: conditional classical sampling model

This additive reference computes the exact classical law of X-index subset selection after first-passage quota stopping, assuming independent basis events and independent uniform selection randomness. It distinguishes conditioning on (i) preselection public basis history and eligible population, (ii) completion before a transmission cap, and (iii) a postselection authenticated transcript revealing indices. A fixed eligible population has a uniform size-n subset distribution; a mixture over variable population sizes need not have a uniform rank-subset marginal. The cap changes the population mixture, not the conditional uniformity for a given eligible population under the stated independence assumptions. Once indices are announced, they are fixed public data.

This is not a statement about random sampling of quantum states against an adversary. No device/source model, entropy bound, authentication provisioning, error-reconciliation leakage, finite-key theorem hypotheses, composable secrecy, or HPKE-export authorization is established. No changes to live protocol modules or secrets. All security qualification flags remain false.
